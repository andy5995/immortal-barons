# Send Spy

Send Spy gathers military intelligence on an enemy. It reveals their land,
troops, defenses, gold, and more, so you can plan an attack. If the mission
fails, your spy is caught and does not return.

Send Spy is the one operation where agents do not change your
chance. Neither your agents nor the enemy's count here. Just over half of
all spies get through, whoever you send them against.
