# Planetary Diplomacy

Your board keeps a short list of where it stands with each of the other
planets. Each one is marked **Enemy**, **None**, **Peace** or **Allied**. Open
it from InterPlanetary Operations with **Diplomacy List**. You also see the
mark for a planet whenever you name one, on the line that reads "Our current
relations with...".

Only your board's Coordinator can change the list. It is on the Coordinator
Menu, under Diplomacy Modification.

## What it does and does not do

The list is a note from your Coordinator to the barons on your own board. It
tells you what your board thinks of a planet. It does nothing else:

- It stops nothing. A planet marked Enemy can still trade with you, and you can
  still attack a planet marked Allied.
- The other planets never see it. They keep their own list, and it may say
  something quite different about you.

One thing does use it. When you write to **Allied Planets** from the IP
Messages menu, your message goes to every planet the list marks Allied.

## Using it

Read the list before you commit forces. Your Coordinator marks a planet Enemy
after seeing something you may not have seen — a raid on a neighbour, a strike
on your own board, a message that went badly. It is the cheapest warning you
will get.

If you disagree with a mark, say so. The Coordinator is elected, and the list
follows whoever holds the post.
