# Turns and Protection

You get a set number of turns each day. When you use them all, come back the
next day for more.

The day turns at midnight on the computer that runs the game. That is not always
midnight where you are, so the entry menu shows the game's own time and how long
is left of the day.

New players get New Realm Protection: a number of turns during which you cannot
attack or be attacked. It only ticks down on turns you actually play, so you are
safe while you learn the game.

A realm still under protection shows its letter in square brackets in the lists
of realms — `[C]`, where every other realm shows `(C)` — so you can see who
cannot be attacked yet.
