# Chemical Attack

A Chemical Attack is a gas missile. It ruins fewer regions than a nuclear
missile, and it also kills people. It ruins about a third as many regions as a
nuclear strike and kills a fifth of the enemy's people outright. It also lowers
both their military morale and their popular support. It leaves their troopers alone.

The morale and support damage is the part that lasts. Casualties come back as
the population grows; a realm knocked down to two-thirds support earns less and
fights worse until it has climbed back.

The arms broker prices the missile off the target's population as well as its
regions, so gassing a crowded realm is expensive. Nothing intercepts it — not SDI,
not turrets, not tanks.

Strategy: this is the weapon to use on a rich, crowded rival you cannot beat in
the field. A Nuclear Attack hurts their regions harder, but only a chemical strike
touches the people paying for their army.
