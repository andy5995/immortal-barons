# Doomer Kaboomer

The Doomer Kaboomer is the ultimate weapon. It is aimed at a whole enemy planet
rather than at one baron, and your planet can only have one at a time.

Nobody buys it alone. One baron starts it and names the target, and then any
baron here can put money into it, a million gold at a time. The price depends on
how big the target planet is: aiming at a planet much larger than yours costs
more. When the money is all in, the weapon is finished and waits for the baron
who started it to launch it. They can also scrap it instead, but the gold is
gone either way.

Once launched it takes a couple of days to arrive, and the target planet can see
it the whole time — while it is still being built, and again while it is in the
air. Their barons can send jets at it, and only jets: nothing else can reach it.
Every wave they send takes a piece out of it, and if they send enough it never
arrives. What is left of it when it lands takes a share of every realm's regions
on that planet. SDI reduces the damage.

If a Doomer Kaboomer is ever aimed at your planet, you will hear about it in the
news. Send jets.
