# Troopers

Troopers are your basic soldiers. They fight on both attack and defense, but
they are weak at each — an offense of 1 and a defense of 1.

They are the cheapest unit to buy and cheap to keep. About seven troopers cost
as much as one tank, but a tank has the strength of four troopers. So troopers
are the better value early, and tanks win later.

Troopers eat more food than any other unit, so a large trooper army needs a
strong food supply.

Troopers also help defend your empire against R5-Slappenheimer missile strikes.
They are a common target of enemy covert operations: enemy agents can make your
troopers desert.

Buy troopers early to survive, then shift toward tanks as your economy grows.
