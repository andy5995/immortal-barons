# Jets

Jets attack only. They have an offense of 2 and a defense of 0, so they add
nothing to your defense. One jet hits about twice as hard on attack as a
trooper.

Jets are cheap to buy but expensive to keep, so do not stockpile more than you
will use.

Jets cannot reach a battle on their own — you need carriers to carry them. One
carrier moves up to 100 jets. Send enough carriers or your jets stay home.

An enemy SDI shield cuts your jets' attacking strength by about a quarter to a
third, so jets are weaker against a well-shielded target.

On the ground jets are vulnerable: in a regular attack the enemy's bombers
destroy your grounded jets, and enemy turrets shoot down jets that do attack.
