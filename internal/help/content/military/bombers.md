# Bombers

Bombers have no attack or defense strength of their own — an offense of 0 and a
defense of 0. Their job is to carry payloads.

Missiles and interplanetary bombings need bombers to deliver them. Without
enough bombers, you cannot launch those attacks.

In a regular attack, your bombers first destroy the enemy jets that are sitting
on the ground, weakening the enemy's next strike.

Bombers add a lot to your net worth, so a fleet of them raises your score as
well as your reach.
