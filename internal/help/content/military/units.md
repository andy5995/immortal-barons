# Units

Your army is built from these units. Each one has a job.

## Troopers

Troopers fight on both attack and defense, but they are weak at each. They are
cheap to buy and cheap to keep. They eat more food than other units. They help
you defend against Sabre missile attacks.

## Jets

Jets attack only. They cannot defend. One jet is about twice as strong on
attack as a trooper. Jets are cheap to buy but cost a lot to keep. They need
carriers to reach a battle: one carrier moves 100 jets. An enemy SDI shield
cuts your jets' strength by about a quarter.

## Turrets

Turrets defend only. One turret is about twice as strong on defense as a
trooper. They shoot down attacking jets, destroy tanks, and kill troops. They
also help stop incoming missiles. Covert operations cannot destroy them.

## Tanks

Tanks are the best all-around unit. They are strong on both attack and defense.
They cost a lot to buy but little to keep. A strong HeadQuarters and good morale
make your tanks even stronger. Tanks help you defend against chemical attacks.

## Bombers

Bombers have no attack or defense strength of their own. They carry payloads.
Missiles and interplanetary bombings need bombers to deliver them. In a regular
attack, your bombers destroy the enemy jets that are sitting on the ground.

## Carriers

Carriers move your jets to a battle and carry goods for trade. One carrier moves
up to 100 jets.

## Covert Agents

Agents run covert operations, such as spying and sabotage. Having many agents
also helps defend you against enemy covert operations.

## HeadQuarters

A HeadQuarters coordinates your army. A strong HQ makes your tanks much more
effective. Enemies can bomb your HQ to weaken your tanks.
