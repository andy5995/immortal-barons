# Tanks

Tanks are the best all-around unit, strong on both attack and defense — an
offense of 4 and a defense of 4, the strength of four troopers in one unit.

They cost a lot to buy but little to keep. A tank army is expensive to build and
cheap to maintain, so over a long game tanks are the most efficient force.

A strong HeadQuarters and high military morale make your tanks even more
effective, so keep both up. Enemies will bomb your HQ to weaken your tanks.

Tanks also help defend your empire against chemical missile attacks.

Grow your economy first, then convert your gold into tanks for a force that
wins both attacks and defenses.
