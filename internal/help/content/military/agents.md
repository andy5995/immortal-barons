# Covert Agents

Agents are your spies. They carry out covert operations — spying, sabotage,
bombing enemy targets, bribery, and more.

You spend agents on operations. Each operation needs one agent, and a failed
operation loses it. On your own planet, the number of agents on either side does
not change your chance of success, unless a treaty adds an ally's agents. The
Covert Operations topic explains what does. Against terrorist operations between
planets, the target's agents do help it defend.

A large stock of agents lets you keep working after several failures. Agents do not fight in normal battles. See the Covert
Operations topic for what they can do.
