# Covert Agents

Agents are your spies. They run covert operations — spying, sabotage, bombing
enemy targets, bribery, and more.

Your success at a covert operation depends on how many agents you have compared
to the target. More agents means a better chance.

Keeping many agents also defends you: it lowers the chance that enemy covert
operations against you succeed.

Agents do not fight in normal battles. Build a network of them if you plan to
wage a secret war. See the Covert Operations topic for what they can do.
