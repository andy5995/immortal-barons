# Turrets

Turrets defend only. They have a defense of 2 and an offense of 0 — about twice
as strong on defense as a trooper. They are the defensive counterpart to jets.

Turrets shoot down attacking jets, destroy attacking tanks, and kill attacking
troops. They also help intercept incoming nuclear missiles.

Covert operations cannot destroy your turrets, which makes them a stable core
for your defense.

Some strategy guides call this unit a "Missile Base." It is the same defensive
unit.

Build turrets to hold your land, and pair them with troopers and tanks so you
can both defend and strike back.
