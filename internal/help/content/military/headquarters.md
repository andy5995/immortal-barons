# HeadQuarters

Your HeadQuarters coordinates your army. It is a one-time building project that
advances a little each turn until it is complete.

While it is being built — and more so once finished — it raises how much your
Tanks add to both attack and defense. Enemies can bomb your HQ with covert
operations to weaken your tanks.
