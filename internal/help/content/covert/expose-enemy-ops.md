# Expose Enemy Ops

Expose Enemy Ops is a defensive operation. It shields your empire from all
incoming covert operations for a short time. Use it when you expect an enemy to
strike.

Anything the shield turns aside counts as a caught agent, so on top of blocking
the damage you find out which realm sent it.
