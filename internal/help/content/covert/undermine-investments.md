# Undermine Investments

Undermine Investments is an InterPlanetary operation, on the Special Operations
menu. It damages the bank investments of every realm on another planet that are
due within a few days, trimming a few percent off the money they were due to
get back. Most runs are driven off before they reach it and do nothing.
