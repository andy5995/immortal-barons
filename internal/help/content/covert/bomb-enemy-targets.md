# Bomb Enemy Targets

Bomb Enemy Targets sends your agents to plant bombs across an enemy realm. You
choose the realm and nothing else: if the operation lands, your agency picks one
of the enemy's holdings at random and destroys part of it. It may hit their
people, their troopers, their own agents, their tanks, their jets, or their food
store.

Food is the prize. A strike that reaches the granaries burns a large share of it,
far more than the few percent the other five lose, so a realm already short of
food can be pushed into starvation by a single lucky raid. You cannot aim for it,
though, so the operation is a gamble rather than a plan.
