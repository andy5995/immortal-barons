# Free Trade Agreement

A Free Trade Agreement lets people and goods move freely, with no border tax. You
earn a lot from income taxes — nearly twice what a Tariff Trade Agreement pays,
on the same count of the smaller realm's people.

People mixing across borders spread low morale and low support. Each day the
healthier of the two realms drifts down toward the worse one, as far as its
figure and no further. The drift runs one way only: the worse realm is never
lifted by the contact. Signing with an unhappy neighbor gives you the income,
but it can lower your own morale and support. Check their figures as well as your
own.
