# Free Trade Agreement

A Free Trade Agreement lets people and goods move freely, with no border tax. You
earn a lot from income taxes — nearly twice what a Tariff Trade Agreement pays,
on the same count of the smaller realm's people. But people mixing across borders
can spread low morale and low support, so watch it.
