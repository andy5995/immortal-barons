# Treaties and Alliances

Treaties are agreements you send to other rulers. A treaty starts only when the
other side accepts it. Each treaty type has its own topic.

Two related actions also live on the Diplomacy menu. A Declaration of War ends an
agreement without causing trouble at home; the treaty is not truly broken until
the other realm is told. View Treaties shows your current relations with the
other rulers in the game.
