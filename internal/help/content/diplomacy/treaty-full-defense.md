# Full Defense Alliance

A Full Defense Alliance is the strongest alliance. You commit a large part of
your troopers and tanks to defend an ally in need. It works only against attacks from
your own planet. It gives no help against a strike from another planet.

Those forces can die in a battle you were not part of. When that happens you
are told what you lost, who you were defending, and who attacked them, so watch
your report after an ally has been attacked.
