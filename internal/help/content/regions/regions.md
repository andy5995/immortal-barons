# Regions

Regions are the territory your realm holds. You buy them on the Spending menu,
and the price rises as you buy more. There are eight region types you can buy,
each with a different job, and Waste, which only a strike creates. Each type has
its own topic.

The realm that holds the most regions is the Planetary Master.

Every region you own costs gold to maintain, every turn. The cost is the same
for all region types, and it is large. It is often the biggest bill you pay.
Technology regions lower the cost.
