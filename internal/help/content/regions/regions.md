# Regions

Land is made of regions. You buy regions from a market, and the price rises as
you buy more. There are eight region types, each with a different job. Each type
has its own topic.

Every region you own costs gold to maintain, every turn. The cost is the same
for all region types, and it is large. It is often the biggest bill you pay.
Technology regions lower the cost.
