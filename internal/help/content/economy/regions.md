# Regions

Land is made of regions. You buy regions from a market, and the price rises as
you buy more. There are eight region types, each with a different job.

## Coastal

Best for tourism income. In good times it makes the most gold. But with low
public support, your people lose interest and tourism drops sharply.

## Mountain

Mines natural resources. Income is lower on average, but steady — few things
change it. Mountains also boost how much your Industrial regions produce.

## Desert

Good for solar power. Income swings with conditions, but on average it beats
mountains and trails good Coastal land.

## River

Provides a steady river income.

## Agricultural

Grows food. This is your main source of food if you are not buying it.

## Urban

Home to your people. More urban regions means more people can live in your
empire.

## Industrial

Builds military each turn. In some games this is your only way to get new units.
A good base of Mountain regions makes your industry produce more.

## Technology

Raises your efficiency. A larger share of Technology regions boosts your income
and your army and lowers some upkeep. Bigger empires need more Technology to get
the same benefit.
