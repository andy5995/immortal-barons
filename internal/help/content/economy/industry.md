# Industry

Each Industrial region makes gold and production points every turn. The points
are built into units.

Use Set Industries to split your production across unit types by percentage. You
can change the split at any time.

Specialize marks one unit type as your focus. It does not replace your
percentage split. Any production your percentages leave unspent goes to the
specialized unit. Specializing cannot be undone, so choose with care.
